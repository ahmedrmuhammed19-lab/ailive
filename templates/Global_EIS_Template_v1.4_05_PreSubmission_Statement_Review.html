APP BAppendix B — Methodology, Rule 5 & Glossary

Intelligence source: eis_analysis_functions.py → revision_notes crosscheck_text

How this review must be filled, and the vocabulary it uses.

TermMeaning in this review
BBF / BCFBalance brought / carried forward — the printed anchors that split a statement into months
Unnamed railETRANSFER row whose counterparty prints as null — money moved but the bank names nobody
Alias onlyCounterparty name truncated by the bank — identity must be confirmed from a document
Round-trip candidateSame-day opposite legs of near-equal amount — potential balance dressing, never auto-deducted
Rule 5Firm doctrine: candidates go to an audit log; nothing is deducted until a reviewer confirms it
Proven salaryCredit whose statement text carries a Salary/Payroll label — the only income that counts as salary
Adjusted viewExternal-only figures after reviewer-confirmed audit-log deductions (identical until confirmed)
Late build-upCredits landing in the final days before the closing balance — what built the last page
Avg daily balanceMean end-of-day balance across the window — the sustained-funds view assessors trust
CoverageBalance ÷ baseline threshold — printed both for closing and average daily balance

Methodology: figures are exact to the piastre; no "~", "Est." or "approx." is produced. The balance chain is verified per transaction before any intelligence is computed, and every aggregate in this template reconciles to the same verified ledger. Revision discipline: when a figure changes between revisions, revision_notes() prints the old value, the new value and the difference — totals never move silently.
