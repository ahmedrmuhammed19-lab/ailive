How to read the matrix

The light-blue row is the case's active benchmark (here: the joint two-traveller UK line). Coverage = closing balance ÷ required funds. Every line the matrix carries must show its measured required value and multiple; the authenticity line is informational and always shows its screening basis.

4 · Required Client Actions — numbered gate cards

The conclusion section now ships as numbered action gates (analysis cards in the standard grid): each gate names one document the client must obtain before submission. Six-gate reference set shown.

Gate 1 — Funding source. Identify the third-party customer behind internal injections: copy of their statement first page + relationship proof + short sponsor letter.
Gate 2 — SWIFT wire. Obtain the bank-issued credit advice naming the remitter and purpose for the large inward wire.
Gate 3 — Large purchase. Contract/receipt for the major outflow, plus registration if the asset is to be declared.
Gate 4 — Obligations schedule. Loan statement for the facility and payee references for the recurring cheque pair.
Gate 5 — Name consistency. Confirm passport romanisation of the holder's name and align it across the visa file and cover letter.
Gate 6 — Co-traveller file. Second traveller's passport details, marriage certificate as relationship evidence, and an itinerary naming both travellers.
